04 · Rights and duties

WHS is a system of duties, not an HR task.

TechNova Solutions is the PCBU. Its duty follows the work — into the office, home workspaces and contractor interactions.

Primary duty

Ensure health and safety, including psychological health, so far as is reasonably practicable.

TechNova
PCBU
Safe workEnvironment, plant, structures and systems without health and safety risks.
InformationTraining, instruction and supervision workers can understand and use.
ConsultationWorkers contribute when hazards, risks and controls are considered.
Monitor + reviewCheck worker health, workplace conditions and control effectiveness.
TechNova is the PCBU and holds the primary duty. Officers exercise due diligence, while workers take reasonable care, follow reasonable instructions and cooperate with procedures. The Essential Energy course case also reinforces that PCBU responsibilities can extend across contractor and subcontractor arrangements. Most importantly, consultation is two-way: workers help shape controls because they understand the work.

Urgent hazardProposed same-day triage and interim control
Routine concernProposed acknowledgement within two business days
The experience is designed around trust. A report must be quick, visibly owned and closed out with the worker. The response times shown are proposed service standards, not statutory timeframes. Immediate danger always follows the emergency process.

08 · Implementation

Start small. Fix what matters. Prove it works.

Stabilise
Days 0–30
  • Remove obvious cable hazards
  • Refresh wardens and emergency contacts
  • Consult workers and validate risk ratings
  • Launch minimum viable reporting page
Outcome: urgent risk is visible and owned
Equip
Days 31–60
  • Run workstation assessments
  • Provide equipment and set-up guidance
  • Brief leaders and all staff
  • Publish rights, duties and resources
Outcome: safe practice becomes easier
Improve
Days 61–90
  • Review reports and repeat hazards
  • Test emergency arrangements
  • Survey remote and office workers
  • Refine controls and content
Outcome: evidence guides the next cycle

We recommend a 90-day pilot. The first month stabilises immediate issues and opens the reporting channel. The second equips workers and publishes the complete website. The third reviews evidence, tests controls and improves the system with workers.

Current-law note

The supplied student documents refer to the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2017. That regulation was repealed and replaced by the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2025, which commenced on 22 August 2025.

This presentation uses the current 2025 Regulation while retaining the supplied project's risk matrix and learning objectives.

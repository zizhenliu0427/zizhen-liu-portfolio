Reliable chat under load
Real-time chat improvements in MediaJira during the Codritium internship.
1. Idempotent sends, a transactional outbox and PgBouncer connection pooling.
2. At 100 concurrent users, delivery rose from 83–89% to 9,900 out of 9,900 messages.
3. WebSocket p95 fell from 47.5 seconds to 5.0 seconds.

Collaborative spreadsheets
Multi-user spreadsheet collaboration in MediaJira.
1. Django Channels supports live synchronisation.
2. Presence, remote cursors and collaborative edits.
3. The existing portfolio reports approximately 155ms peer updates.

Migrations, recovery & CI
Cross-module migration and delivery improvements during the Codritium internship.
1. A slug-URL migration spanning 373 files and 12 modules, alongside IDOR fixes.
2. CI fell from 57 to around 20 minutes, with 597 skipped tests recovered.
3. Restored 144 database migrations to recover a production 521 outage.

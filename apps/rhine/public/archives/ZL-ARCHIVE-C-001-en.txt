This personal website grew from open-source generosity, an appreciation of Rhine Lab’s visual world, and collaboration between a person and AI. This final archive is a thank-you to those inspirations and contributions.

Engineering notes
1. Thank you to LBEILC for sharing RhineLabUI, providing an open-source foundation to learn from and build upon.
2. Thank you to HYPERGRYPH and the creators of Arknights. Rhine Lab’s visual design, glass archives and research-terminal atmosphere inspired this interface.
3. Thank you to OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra and Codex for supporting implementation, debugging, modelling scripts and ongoing iteration.
4. This tribute retains the original Rhine glass, twin-ring interior and RHINE LAB lettering as a nod to the original work and its open-source adaptation.

Open-source and visual origins
From the original visual work to its open-source recreation and this personal adaptation, each contribution deserves acknowledgement.
1. RhineLabUI: the open-source interface by LBEILC, providing the foundation for this website’s interface and 3D interactions.
